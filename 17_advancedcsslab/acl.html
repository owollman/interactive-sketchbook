At first I couldn't figure out how to get the translate feature to work because I was using multiple units of measurements (deg, px, s, etc.) and so I literally just put the numbers which isn't going to do anything because they don't know how far the object is supposed to move on the page. I used all 4 transforms (skew,rotate, translate, and scale). I like being able to set the duration and I found that I like a shorter transition time, about 2-10 seconds, because you get the results quicker. Using multiple transforms gave each element a more polished feel. I felt like just putting a scale change or a rotate made it feel less developed than I wanted. On star 4 I added a 200deg skew on both sides and a 500deg rotate which caused the star to rotate and skew rapidly several times and I think it even changed scale at one point despite me not entering that. I think I like this star the most and the ineteractions between the multiple transforms.
